In 2024 we noticed a continued trend in hiring contingent workers with the help of shift work marketplaces. These marketplaces address a significant portion of the labor market, especially the healthcare, light industrial, and hospitality industries. As varied as these industries are, they have a common cause in ensuring management and operations teams have the necessary tools to plan and verify work, visualize work being done in real time, and automate tasks where possible.
