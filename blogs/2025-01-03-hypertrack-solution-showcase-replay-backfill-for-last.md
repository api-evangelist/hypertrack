Last month, on December 13, we hosted a solution showcase to highlight the nuances of backfilling last minute cancellations in shift work. This 30-minute solution showcase was led by HyperTrack’s VP of Product, Ram Kakkad and VP of Engineering, Saurabh Das. HyperTrack validates over 1 million shifts per month, the solution showcase discusses best practices implementations of the tech and tools that allow shift work marketplaces to ensure each shift is successful.
